# AI-only company fundamentals feedback

Handoff and Holdings Review reuse the existing Codex ChatGPT account provider. No API key, provider switch or new credential was added. Holdings offers Rule-based and AI-based selectors with per-account remembered choice. Submitted persistent jobs retain the selected mode; cancellation and saved-output reopening follow existing job ownership and permissions.

Only AI-selected stock/company analysis fetches fundamentals. The shared provider reads current NSE Integrated Filing metadata, selects the latest dated exact-symbol financial filing (Consolidated preferred within the same reporting period), and verifies symbol, period, INR and source unit before extracting supported reported facts from the official rendered filing. It converts Lakhs/Crores/Rupees/Millions explicitly to INR crore, preserves stored precision, and displays at most two decimals. It caches successful/partial evidence 24 hours and unavailable evidence six hours. Requests have bounded size/time, fixed official host allowlists, no redirects and at most metadata plus one filing fetch per uncached company. A filing older than 180 days is marked stale.

Feedback covers reported profitability and financial-health limits, growth, valuation, key risks and supports/conflicts/mixed/insufficient interpretation. Missing comparatives, cash flow, balance-sheet solvency and TTM valuation are unavailable rather than invented. Technical momentum is never called fundamentals. Long-term financial context does not automatically invalidate a short-term trade. Existing conviction, holding action, Auto eligibility, risk, quantities and execution settings are unchanged by financial feedback.

NSE company identity is required; unmapped BSE companies and unresolved/noncompany contracts do not receive guessed ratios. No private account identifiers, quantities, acquisition prices or P&L enter AI holdings evidence. Saved snapshots retain period, filing/retrieval timestamps, source URLs and source hash. Legacy AI snapshots say unavailable; deterministic reports omit fundamentals. Current verified example: Voltas Consolidated quarter ended 30 June 2026, filed 14 August 2026; NSE reports revenue INR4673.50 crore, total profit/loss INR212.76 crore, EPS INR6.46. Growth comparison, TTM valuation and full solvency are incomplete, so this is partial evidence.
