Future failures: DeltaBroker.place classifies failures while holding the native submission lock. Only absence of a native intent during that same attempted submission can generate a durable account/order-scoped no-send receipt. On reconciliation, or asynchronously after startup if a receipt and pending intent coexist, fresh same-account authentication, complete active-order inventory, exact native client-order absence and zero product exposure are mandatory. Unknown acknowledgements with a native intent, missing receipts, changed identity, broker timeouts and contradictory exposure remain blocked. No blind retry or clearing based only on a missing order. Recovery never rearms trading. A previously requested Stop finishes; otherwise explicit Start is required after review.
