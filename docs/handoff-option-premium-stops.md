# Handoff option diagnostics and premium stops

New long-option analyses retain rejected contract identity, master lot/tick, selected expiry, bid/ask, measured spread, OI, volume and Greeks. Only failed checks appear in the reason. Missing Greeks are named individually rather than appended to every rejection. FYERS direct analysis and the reusable account-owned analysis engine both preserve this evidence in saved jobs. Old reports are retained as originally generated.

For a validated bought option, the proposed underlying invalidation is converted into an estimated premium stop using exact strike/expiry, entry ask, chain spot and broker IV. A zero-rate/dividend Black-Scholes scenario reprices the exact option at the underlying stop with unchanged time and IV. The scenario/entry model-price ratio is anchored to the quoted entry premium. Delta/Gamma and the local Taylor estimate are retained as audit evidence; the Taylor approximation does not determine the stop over large distances. Theta/Vega are retained, with zero elapsed-time and IV-change assumptions. Missing or inconsistent finite Greeks, expired contracts, wrong-side invalidation, nonpositive model values or invalid tick rounding withhold the estimate. No default premium percentage is substituted.

Both direct and saved reports distinguish underlying stop, estimated premium stop, underlying entry and option-premium target. Accepted invalidation recomputation supplies a new estimate. Legacy saved reports without an estimate say it is unavailable. The selected target's existing full-premium-risk basis and sizing controls remain unchanged. The paid premium remains the contractual maximum option loss excluding costs; the model stop is not guaranteed execution or confirmed broker protection. Existing ticket preview recalculates executable protection using fresh data; this change does not place or alter orders.

Primary modelling reference: https://www.optionseducation.org/referencelibrary/faq/option-price-behavior and https://prd-web.optionseducation.org/advancedconcepts/gamma. Price depends on spot, strike, time, IV, interest and dividend assumptions; Delta/Gamma are local sensitivities.
