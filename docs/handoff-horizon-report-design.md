# Handoff contract and horizon report

Staged report-only schema and shared renderer separate exact validated contract identity (symbol, strike, CE/PE, expiry, broker source) from underlying-price and option-premium entries, targets and stops. Intraday and swing are separate sections. Existing shared Daily structure and legacy records are retained in an explicitly labelled original/current-plan disclosure; old prices are not relabelled as new horizon calculations. Rejected candidate contracts are labelled rejected rather than selected, with failed checks and unavailable plans.

The user approved the report-only horizon policy on 25 October 2026. Intraday uses completed 15-minute close/EMA20/ATR; swing uses completed Daily close/EMA20/ATR. From EMA20 and close minus ATR for bullish or close plus ATR for bearish, choose the nearest positive level strictly on the adverse side of the quoted underlying entry. If none is valid, that horizon is unavailable. Target equals entry plus/minus selected positive R:R times that horizon's own stop distance; nonpositive or missing targets remain unavailable. Missing/stale timeframe evidence is never borrowed from the other horizon.

Premium entry is the validated exact contract ask. Each horizon's premium target and stop use the existing quote-anchored constant-IV Black-Scholes model at its own underlying levels, same exact strike/side/expiry and valuation time, zero interest/dividend assumptions. Stops round upward and targets downward to the contract tick; invalid or missing Greek/expiry/model evidence withholds the affected value. Time and IV are held unchanged. Horizons identify candle structure rather than predicting time-to-target; swing scenarios never imply holding beyond expiry. Original order-planning levels, premium-target basis, lots and execution settings are retained separately and are not replaced by these report scenarios.

Completed stock analysis, completed index/commodity analysis and generic restored terminal outputs have native disclosure controls, compact by default, with account-scoped expansion preference. Selecting a candidate opens its completed stock plan. Active progress is outside these collapsed containers. Full-page Saved Jobs browsing, chronology, access gates and records are preserved.
