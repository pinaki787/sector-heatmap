# Report-only option outcome estimates

Each independently calculated intraday and swing horizon now captures selected report lots, exact validated contract master lot size, and quantity. Both bought Calls and bought Puts use `(scenario exit premium - entry premium) * quantity`. Underlying point differences never substitute for option P&L. The horizon policy remains completed 15-minute or Daily EMA20/ATR structure, with selected reward:risk targets and quote-anchored IV/Greeks premium mapping.

FYERS uses the existing FYERS_OPTIONS_2026_10_V2 Standard option fee model, verified against published FYERS charges and NSE STT effective 1 April 2026. The estimate assumes one BUY entry and one SELL exit for the entire quantity, INR20 Standard brokerage per executed order, segment exchange fees, clearing, IPFT, SEBI, sell STT, buy stamp, and GST. Account Prime discounts are unverified. Slippage, extra split orders, account-specific charges, exercise and delivery remain unknown and excluded, never assumed zero. Net is after listed fees only.

Other brokers without a verified selected fee model show gross outcomes and withhold fees/net. Missing contract identity, expiry, lot size, invalid lots, stale horizon evidence or missing premium scenarios withhold affected values. Legacy saved reports are not retroactively recalculated. Reports capture the existing Lots per stock input; changing that field requires Collect matches again to save a new outcome snapshot. Existing order and runner settings, order-planning prices, visibility permissions and completed-output collapse behavior are preserved.

Validation includes bought Call/Put arithmetic, three-lot scaling, a known INR60.74 round-trip fee fixture, gross-only unknown costs, missing/stale/invalid evidence, unchanged proposal sizing, legacy rendering, mobile responsive layout and saved report rendering. Activation preserves nine canonical state-file hashes and requires flat broker/stopped-runner checks.
